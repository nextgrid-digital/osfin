## Thesis

Every payment creates context: records, policies, exceptions, and decisions that generic AI cannot reliably connect.

Osfin enables agentic financial operations intelligence that understands this context, coordinates agentic workflows, and turns every exception into a clearer next action.

```
payment event
    │
    ├─► records
    ├─► policies
    ├─► exceptions
    └─► decisions
            │
            └─► governed next action
```

## Products

A connected operating system for reconciliation, disputes, compliance, risk, and financial close.

### Settlement Mesh

Reconciliation infrastructure that connects payment records across banks, gateways, processors, and internal ledgers.

### Exception Resolution

Dispute and chargeback management that carries contested payments from evidence to action.

### Control Views

Reporting and compliance intelligence that turns operational data into accountable views.

### Risk Signals

AML reporting and fraud prevention that surfaces suspicious patterns across payment activity.

### Close Orchestration

Financial close management that coordinates checks, approvals, and sign-off.

```
banks ──┐
gateways ├──► Settlement Mesh ──► ledgers
processors┘         │
                    ├─► Exception Resolution
                    ├─► Control Views
                    ├─► Risk Signals

```
